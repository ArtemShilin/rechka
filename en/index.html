Rechka

A fast messenger for talking to your colleagues. It connects to your organization's Rocket.Chat server: chats, threads, files, voice messages and video calls. Free, with no ads and no data collection.

Download

  • Windows 10 and 11 — Microsoft Store (free; no Microsoft account needed)
  • iPhone, iPad and Mac — coming to the App Store

Install from the command line (Windows), e.g. for administrators:

winget install --id 9N2LPV7HCM4V --source msstore --accept-package-agreements --accept-source-agreements

What you need

An account on a Rocket.Chat server, issued by your organization or the server's administrator. On first launch enter the server's address, then your username and password.
