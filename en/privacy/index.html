Rechka Privacy Policy

Draft. Last updated October 21, 2026.

Rechka is a free client app for Rocket.Chat. It connects to your organization's Rocket.Chat server and shows your chats. The app is made by an individual developer who is not affiliated with Rocket.Chat Technologies or with your organization.

In short

  • The developer runs no servers your messages pass through. Everything you write goes only to the Rocket.Chat server whose address you entered when signing in.
  • The app collects no analytics, shows no ads and shares no data with third parties for tracking.
  • Your organization stores and processes the data on its Rocket.Chat server under its own rules.

Data the app handles

On your device:

  • the server address and your sign-in token, in the Keychain (iPhone, iPad, Mac) or Credential Manager (Windows);
  • loaded messages, photos, files, voice and video messages, in a temporary cache so they show faster;
  • app settings: theme, language, chat folders, sounds, blocked users.

Signing out deletes the file and picture cache, temporary files and the call window's cookies.

On your organization's Rocket.Chat server (what you do in the app is sent there): messages, files, reactions, status, and device details for the Devices list (model, system and app version).

Third-party services

The app contacts them only as described:

  • Jitsi Meet, for video calls: the server named in your Rocket.Chat settings or entered by you in Settings → Chats; when neither is set, the public meet.jit.si. A call opens only when you start or join it.
  • ipwho.is, only if you turn on "Show city by IP address" in Settings → Privacy (off by default). The IP addresses of this device and of your sign-ins in the Devices list are then sent to ipwho.is to show their city.
  • Apple / Microsoft: notifications and system services work under Apple's and Microsoft's terms.

Diagnostics

iPhone and Mac collect crash and freeze reports (MetricKit) and hand them to the app. The app keeps them on the device and never sends them by itself. You can send them to the developer only by hand: Settings → General → Send diagnostics. They hold no messages, names, passwords or tokens — only technical details of the problem, the app version and the device model.

Permissions

Camera, microphone, photos and notifications are asked for only when needed: for calls, voice and video messages, sending photos and new-message notifications. You can withdraw them at any time in the system settings.

Children

The app is meant for organizations' work chats and is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions about the data on the Rocket.Chat server: your organization's administrator. Questions about the app: the developer, via Settings → General → Contact developer.
